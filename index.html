The Story

I started in marketing. Data made me a sharper analyst.

I have a Computer Science background, but my career began in marketing. Within six months of joining, my manager departed — and I found myself solely responsible for the entire marketing function of a B2B technology company.

That experience taught me the most important lesson of my career: the decisions that move the needle are always backed by evidence. Not assumptions, not instinct — data that tells a clear story and points to a specific course of action.

So I went deeper. I built competency in SQL, Python, and Power BI — not to become a data engineer, but to become a sharper analyst. Someone who could identify the right question, find the relevant data, and communicate findings to leadership in a way that leads to action.

I am particularly interested in roles where I can research business problems, analyse user or market behaviour, and build strategies informed by evidence — whether that sits in a business analysis, product marketing, or growth function.
