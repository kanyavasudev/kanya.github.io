My Story

I came for marketing.
I stayed for the data.

I started my career in digital marketing, but I quickly became more interested in the numbers behind campaigns than the campaigns themselves.

I wanted to understand why something worked, why it failed, and what patterns existed beneath user behavior.

That curiosity pushed me toward analytics, reporting, behavioral analysis, and data storytelling.

Today I combine marketing thinking with analytical problem-solving to uncover insights that actually help businesses make decisions.
