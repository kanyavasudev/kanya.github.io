I started in marketing. Data gave me a stronger strategic perspective.

With a Computer Science background and an early career in marketing, I quickly took ownership of the marketing function independently, managing content strategy, SEO, reporting, campaigns, and team coordination.

That's when something clicked. The most impactful business and marketing decisions were consistently backed by data and performance insights.

So I went deeper. I upskilled in SQL, Python, and Power BI — built real projects, analyzed real data, and started asking questions nobody else was asking. The loneliness patterns in 25K+ survey responses. The ridership shifts in 3 years of Chennai Metro data. The behavioral patterns in 50K+ ride bookings.

I combine analytical thinking with marketing strategy and communication, where business problems meet data solutions, and where strategy gets built on evidence.
